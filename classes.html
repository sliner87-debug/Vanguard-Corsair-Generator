Class Features

Spatial Tear
As a standard action, tear a rift in space up to the indicated distance. You can teleport yourself and up to one willing creature. Does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

Warp-Step Strike
Whenever you teleport, your next melee or ranged attack before the end of your turn gains a +4 circumstance bonus to attack and damage.
Evasion
If you make a successful Reflex saving throw against an attack that normally deals half damage on a successful save, you instead take no damage.
Fold Space
Fold the fabric of reality. You can instantly move between two points within range as a move action. If used to bypass a solid object, you must succeed on a Will save (DC 15 + 1 per 5 ft of thickness).
Bonus Magick Talent
You gain a bonus feat or Magick Talent from your approved class list.
Uncanny Dodge

You emit a 30 ft aura that prevents any teleportation or extraplanar travel from enemies unless they succeed on a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your level + Int modifier).
Phase Shift
